# HR Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

## Overview
This project presents an **HR Analytics Dashboard** built using **Power BI** to analyze employee data and identify key factors influencing employee attrition. The dashboard provides interactive visual insights into workforce demographics, salary distribution, job roles, and attrition trends.

The goal of this project is to help organizations make **data driven HR decisions**, reduce employee turnover, and understand workforce behavior.

---

## Key Metrics

- **Total Employees:** 1416  
- **Total Attrition:** 229  
- **Attrition Rate:** 16.2%  
- **Average Age:** 37 years  
- **Average Salary:** 6.5K  
- **Average Years at Company:** 7.0  

---

## Dashboard Insights

### 1. Attrition by Education Field
- Highest attrition observed in **Life Sciences and Medical fields**
- Lower attrition in **Marketing, Technical Degree, and Other fields**

### 2. Attrition by Age Group
- Highest attrition in **26 to 35 age group**
- Lower attrition in employees above **46 years**

### 3. Attrition by Job Role
Major roles affected:
- Laboratory Technician  
- Sales Executive  
- Research Scientist  
- Sales Representative  

### 4. Attrition by Salary Slab
- Majority attrition in **salary up to 5K**
- Attrition decreases as salary increases

### 5. Attrition by Gender
- Male attrition higher than Female

### 6. Attrition by Years at Company
- Most attrition happens within **first few years**
- Employees with long tenure show lower attrition

### 7. Department Level Insights
- Analysis across **HR, Sales, and Research & Development**
- Shows education distribution and workforce composition
